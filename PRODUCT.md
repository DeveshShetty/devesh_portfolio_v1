## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters and engineering leads hiring ML / AI / data engineers in the Irish (and wider European) job market. They skim quickly, compare many candidates, and want fast, credible proof of seniority and production impact. Secondary: UCD contacts and research-minded reviewers who check the publications.

Personal portfolio for Devesh Shetty, a Senior ML & AI Engineer (5 years) relocating to Dublin for an MSc in Computer Science (Negotiated Learning) at University College Dublin, Sept 2026 - Aug 2027. Success: get interviews and a job in the Irish market, and stand out from other candidates. The visitor should leave knowing what he has shipped and how to reach him.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before personality: lead with measured outcomes a hiring manager can verify in seconds.
2. Memorable in one viewport: a recruiter who sees only the first screen should recall one specific thing about Devesh an hour later.
3. Honest about seniority and scope: claims stay exactly as the owner's profile states them.
4. Easy to act on: contact, resume, GitHub and LinkedIn are never more than one tap away.
