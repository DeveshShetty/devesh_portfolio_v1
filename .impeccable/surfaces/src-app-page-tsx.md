# Surface: Devesh Shetty portfolio (single page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Irish and European hiring managers and recruiters skimming for ML / AI / data engineers. Primary action: email Devesh; resume, LinkedIn, GitHub secondary. Constraints: Next.js 15 + Tailwind + framer-motion; section order fixed (hero, about, experience, skills, projects, contact); facts and numbers stay as written in PRODUCT.md; reduced-motion respected; no eyebrow labels, no gradient text, no section numbers. Replacement world: the old instrument-panel look is anti-reference ("wall of data"). Brief pinned by the user: Tesla/Apple cinematic crossed with Gotham noir.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio staged like a film title sequence: one idea per screen, lit by a single searchlight. It refuses the dense dashboard and the glowing-neon AI template.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink ground with soot panels, bone-white type, one amber signal (#FFB000) used only for light: beams, the primary action, numerals when lit. Big Shoulders Display (city-signage condensed) at huge scale, Hanken Grotesk for reading. Skyline silhouette, drifting haze, a one-time bat crossing.

STORY: Visitor sees a name lit from behind, learns senior ML and AI engineer, scrolls through six giant results one at a time, then roles, skills, projects, and one lit action: email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh ink. Name stacked at 15vw, left aligned; pointer-driven spotlight lights the letters amber-white; skyline along the bottom with a few flickering windows; role, one sentence, Email me and Resume beneath the name; nav is a thin transparent strip.

FORM: user-pinned brief, not rolled; seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
